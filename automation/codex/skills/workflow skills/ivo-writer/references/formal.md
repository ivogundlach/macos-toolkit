# School and formal mode

Use this reference for personal drafts, notes, essays, application writing, posts, and formal documents.


## Writing quality

- State the thesis or governing claim early and defend it directly.
- Use standard paragraph structure without forcing symmetrical sections.
- Prefer strong, precise vocabulary when it is exact. Avoid ornamental abstraction.
- Connect specific ideas with logical transitions rather than generic transition words.
- Integrate evidence into the reasoning. Do not write `As seen in...` or `This highlights...` as empty bridges.
- Preserve Ivo's real supplied memories, private experiences, local analogies, mixed feelings, and defensible first-person judgments when relevant.
- Never invent an experience, source, quotation, motive, date, number, or outcome to make prose sound more human.
- Express defensible opinions with declarative confidence. Do not add reflexive hedging merely to sound balanced.
- Keep objective facts, genuine uncertainty, and source status accurate. Assertive cadence does not convert an uncertain claim into an established fact.
- Allow an unusually short sentence or paragraph when it carries real weight. Let cadence follow the argument.
- Drop a formulaic summary conclusion when the final substantive paragraph already lands the point.

## Short social posts

- Build the post around a concrete moment, friction, observation, or consequence Ivo supplied. If no truthful anecdote is available, ask for one or write without one; never invent lived experience to make a post sound authentic.
- Make the feeling and its purpose explicit. Let emotion come from what happened and why it mattered, not from generic praise, marketing language, or decorative intensifiers.
- Preserve bluntness, irregular cadence, and distinctive wording when they carry Ivo's voice. Use profanity or emphatic capitalization only when Ivo supplied it, accepted it as a benchmark, or the immediate context clearly supports it; never add either mechanically.
- Prefer the revealing user detail over a product-feature list. A reader should understand what changed for Ivo, not merely what the product claims to do.
- When Ivo provides a draft, revise its sentence structure and length without sanding off its strongest image, emotional force, or point of view. Treat the latest passage he accepted as the local voice benchmark and change only what the new request requires.
- Honor platform or user-specified character limits exactly. Count the complete proposed post, including spaces and line breaks, then tighten syntax and repetition before cutting substance.
- Return one finished post unless Ivo asks for alternatives or commentary.

## Final audit

- Check that every source claim is supported and every quotation is accurate.
- Make repeated names, dates, numbers, terminology, and identifiers agree.
- Remove placeholders, TODO/TBD markers, sample values, duplicated blocks, broken markup, and assistant residue.
- Put the thesis or ask where a first-time reader can find it immediately.
- Break wall-of-text paragraphs and untangle noun piles or long clauses.
- Preserve useful irregularity. Do not polish real personality out of the draft.
- For user-authored personal text, report detector-pattern findings separately; never silently rewrite around them.
