# Style controls

Apply the universal rules to every mode. Apply detector-aware cluster analysis only to the `user_authored_personal` branch defined in [formal.md](formal.md).

## Universal boundaries

- Rewrite; do not amputate. Preserve every material fact, ask, constraint, deadline, identifier, source claim, and implication unless irrelevant, duplicated, or explicitly wrong.
- Rewrite necessary substance plainly instead of deleting it because it sounds synthetic.
- Add specificity only from Ivo's supplied context or verified evidence.
- Never fabricate names, facts, experiences, memories, numbers, motives, sources, quotations, or outcomes.
- Use real private or unrecorded experiences only when Ivo supplied them. Their privacy makes the perspective specific; it does not make invention permissible.
- State defensible opinions directly. Preserve factual uncertainty instead of hedging the opinion itself.

## Ivo's voice

- Default to direct, specific, grounded prose. Distinctive writing does not require a manufactured hook, exaggerated attitude, or theatrical confidence.
- Open with the governing claim when it is already strong. Use a concrete irritation, observation, or consequence only when it improves the argument naturally; never bolt one on for personality.
- Use blunt everyday judgments when they are accurate and proportionate. Preserve charged words Ivo supplied instead of laundering them into corporate language, but do not invent charged language on his behalf.
- Vary cadence according to the thought. Use a fragment, rhetorical question, or direct command only when it sounds natural in the immediate passage, not as a recurring signature.
- Let energetic personal prose move from a constraint to agency when Ivo's supplied experience supports that arc. Do not impose that theme on unrelated writing or application fields.
- Prefer one earned concluding line over a slogan. Ambitious conclusions are allowed, but calm confidence is the baseline; never inflate a modest point into a manifesto.
- Change expression and structure without inventing autobiographical details, prior beliefs, accomplishments, motives, or emotions.
- Do not mechanically insert profanity, slogans, exclamation marks, rhetorical questions, fragments, or grand conclusions.
- Keep routine correspondence socially appropriate. Do not force the heightened cadence of a personal manifesto into a logistical email.

Use both sides of the supplied application benchmark. Its successful passage states the problem directly, explains the role of AI, proposes a practical response, and ends with one earned question. Its rejected opening—two staged rhetorical complaints—felt over-the-top and unnatural. Preserve the reasoning and force of the former; do not imitate the surface attitude of the latter.

## Banned lexicon

Do not use these verbs: `underscore`, `highlight`, `showcase`, `foster`, `enhance`, `bolster`, `epitomize`, `encapsulate`, `delve`.

Do not use these abstract nouns: `tapestry`, `landscape`, `testament`, `interplay`, `intricacies`, `nuances`, `synergy`, `evolution`.

Do not use these adjectives: `pivotal`, `crucial`, `meticulous`, `vibrant`, `robust`, `multifaceted`, `comprehensive`, `enduring`, `groundbreaking`.

Do not begin sentences with `Additionally`, `Moreover`, `Furthermore`, or `Notably`.

Do not use these clichés: `a testament to`, `stands as a reminder`, `marking a shift`, `setting the stage`.

## Structural controls

- Avoid conspicuous lists of exactly three adjectives or parallel phrases.
- Avoid `Not only X, but also Y` and `It is not X, but rather Y` constructions.
- Avoid comma-ended present-participle tails such as `..., creating a new standard.`
- Prefer `is` and `are` over inflated copulas such as `serves as`, `stands as`, or `represents`.
- Do not use vague attribution such as `Experts argue`, `Many believe`, or `Industry reports suggest`. Cite the source or state the supported claim directly.
- Avoid canned `Despite [challenge], [subject] continues to thrive` conclusions.
- Repeat the clearest noun instead of cycling synonyms mechanically.
- Avoid fake `from X to Y` breadth when X and Y are not a real scale.
- Do not fill missing facts with generic speculation such as `maintains a low profile`, `likely grew up`, or `it is believed that`.
- Keep necessary attributive hyphens. Drop unnecessary post-noun hyphens.
- Start headed sections with substance rather than a one-line restatement.
- Unless writing release notes or a changelog, describe the subject as it is instead of narrating the edit history.

## Formatting controls

- Use straight ASCII quotes, not smart quotes.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Do not overuse bold emphasis or em dashes.
- Remove assistant wrappers such as `Here is the draft` and `Let me know if you need changes.`

## Detector-aware false-positive review

This review is allowed only for Ivo-authored, non-assessed personal text after both attestations. It is report-only.

- Look for clusters rather than treating one clean transition, formal word, em dash, triad, or short fragment as proof of anything.
- Preserve real irregularity: odd details, first-person judgments, mixed feelings, dated references, and natural asides.
- Prefer real supplied context over generic semantic predictability.
- Vary cadence according to the argument. A short sentence can land a point; a longer sentence can carry qualification or reasoning.
- Break unnecessary symmetry when the content does not call for it.
- State opinions confidently without falsifying objective claims.

These are local style heuristics. They do not detect AI, certify authorship or originality, guarantee a Pangram/GPTZero/Originality result, or establish compliance with an academic policy. Never automatically rewrite text solely to remove a detector-related flag.
